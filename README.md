# Marketing Mix, Attribution & ROAS Optimization

**Business question:** Which ad channels generate profitable customers, and where should the next Rs 1 lakh of budget go?

> **All data is SYNTHETIC**, generated by `src/gen_p3_data.py` (seed 42). Results show the method, not real-world benchmarks.

## Stack
Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, SQLAlchemy), MySQL 8, pytest

## How it works
1. `src/gen_p3_data.py` creates 92 days x 6 channels of spend, funnel and revenue data, plus a landing-page A/B test.
2. `sql/` builds the channel scorecard (CPL, CAC, ROAS, LTV and LTV:CAC) in MySQL.
3. `src/channel_model.py` fits a response curve per channel: revenue = a x spend^b. A b below 1 means diminishing returns.
4. `src/budget.py` allocates extra budget in Rs 10,000 steps to the channel with the highest marginal ROAS.
5. `src/ab_test.py` runs a two-proportion z-test with a confidence interval and a sample-size check.
6. `tests/` checks the SQL scorecard against pandas, the funnel, the curves and the allocator.

## Results

**Channel scorecard (92 days, total spend Rs 1.12 Cr, blended ROAS 3.29x)**

| Channel | Spend (Rs lakh) | CAC (Rs) | ROAS | Marginal ROAS | LTV:CAC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta Retargeting | 10.0 | 487 | 6.11x | 3.27x | 5.7 |
| Google Search | 31.8 | 705 | 4.03x | 3.01x | 3.4 |
| Meta Prospecting | 26.7 | 827 | 3.22x | 2.49x | 2.3 |
| Influencer | 17.5 | 980 | 2.60x | 2.52x | 2.1 |
| YouTube | 14.1 | 1,283 | 1.94x | 1.81x | 1.3 |
| Google Display | 12.1 | 1,440 | 1.75x | 1.19x | 1.0 |

- **Best average ROAS is not the best place for the next rupee.** Meta Retargeting returns 6.1x on average but only 3.3x on the next rupee (b = 0.53), because it saturates quickly.
- **Google Display (LTV:CAC 1.0) and YouTube (1.3)** barely earn back their cost over 12 months.

**Budget allocation (extra spend per month)**

| Extra budget | Marginal ROAS method | All to best-ROAS channel | Split evenly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rs 1 lakh | +Rs 3.08 lakh revenue | +Rs 3.06 lakh | +Rs 2.37 lakh |
| Rs 5 lakh | +Rs 14.61 lakh | +Rs 12.82 lakh | +Rs 11.67 lakh |

At Rs 1 lakh the two smart strategies are nearly tied. The marginal method pulls ahead (about 14%) as the budget grows, and it moves part of the money into Google Search.

**A/B test (new landing page):** conversion 3.11% to 3.90% (+25% relative), z = 5.24, p < 0.00001, 95% CI for the difference +0.50 to +1.09 points. Detecting a 0.5-point lift needs about 20,365 visitors per variant and the test had about 29,600, so it was adequately powered.

## Recommendation
Move budget gradually towards Google Search and Meta Retargeting, review Google Display and YouTube (LTV:CAC near break-even), and roll out the new landing page. Validate any large budget move with a holdout or geo test first.

## Limitations
- Synthetic data: the response curves are built in, so real data will be noisier and less clean.
- With 92 daily points, each channel's elasticity has real error (R-squared 0.66 to 0.92), so treat the allocation as a direction, not a precise forecast.
- Attribution is last-touch as given; channels interact (for example, prospecting feeds retargeting), which this model ignores.
- Curves assume a constant response over the period (no seasonality or creative fatigue).
- LTV uses average order value, repeat orders and a flat 45% margin per channel.
